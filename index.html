Edge AI • Model Compression

Edge AI Quantization Trade-offs for Mobile Healthcare & Agricultural Diagnostics

Authors: Nur Alam Rakib, et al.

Abstract: Deploying deep convolutional and vision-transformer models on resource-constrained mobile devices requires aggressive quantization. This study systematically evaluates INT8 and mixed-precision quantization trade-offs across latency, memory footprint, and diagnostic accuracy in offline mobile healthcare screening and crop leaf disease detection.

Non-Invasive ABO Blood Grouping via Optical Finger-Photoplethysmography & Machine Learning

Authors: Nur Alam Rakib, et al.

Abstract: Traditional blood typing requires invasive blood draws and chemical reagents. We investigate multi-wavelength optical transmission patterns through peripheral tissue paired with lightweight ensemble and neural classifiers to estimate ABO blood group phenotypes non-invasively.

Undergraduate Thesis Focus 2025 – 2026
Computer Vision • Image Restoration

Lightweight Low-Light Image Enhancement with Noise Suppression for Real-Time Vision

Authors: Nur Alam Rakib

Abstract: Images captured under extreme low-light conditions suffer from severe photon noise, low contrast, and color distortion. This work explores retinex-inspired deep architectures designed to restore natural illumination while preserving edge sharpness at real-time inference speeds.
